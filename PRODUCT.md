# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The initial users are Artyom and Codex working together to inspect and refine a manually classified archive of IB Mathematics AA HL past-paper questions. They need a fast, information-dense interface for auditing topic labels, solution methods, mark allocations, and classification structure.

## Product Purpose

The product turns the classification pilot into a browsable research tool. It should make all questions and subparts visible, filterable, comparable, and easy to trace back to their source paper. Success means that classification mistakes, inconsistent method labels, and taxonomy gaps can be found quickly before automated classification is introduced.

## Brand Personality

Precise, dense, analytical. The interface should feel like a trusted research instrument: calm under heavy information, direct in its language, and rigorous about provenance.

## Anti-references

The product must not resemble a marketing landing page, a decorative grid of dashboard cards, a gamified learning platform, or a generic AI-generated SaaS interface. Decoration must never compete with the underlying mathematical structure.

## Design Principles

1. Make provenance obvious. Every classification must remain connected to paper, question, part, marks, and source pages.
2. Optimize for comparison. Filters and repeated field placement should make patterns and inconsistencies easy to scan.
3. Show method as a sequence. Ordered solution paths and accepted alternatives are more valuable than isolated tags.
4. Preserve information density without visual noise. Hierarchy, alignment, and progressive disclosure should carry complexity.
5. Keep the taxonomy inspectable. Topic and method labels should look like editable research data, not unquestionable system output.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface is Russian-first while official IB terminology, identifiers, and taxonomy keys remain in English. It supports keyboard navigation, visible focus states, high text contrast, reduced motion, and layouts that remain usable under text zoom and on narrow screens.
