# May 2024 TZ1 Paper 1 Question 8 — verified transcription

Question paper, printed page 9 (PDF page 10), maximum 7 marks:

- (a)(i) Find the first two non-zero Maclaurin terms of `sin(x^2)`.
- (a)(ii) Find the first two non-zero Maclaurin terms of `sin^2(x^2)`. Parts (i) and (ii) are worth 5 marks in total.
- (b) Hence, or otherwise, find the first two non-zero Maclaurin terms of `4x sin(x^2) cos(x^2)` [2].

Markscheme PDF pages 18–19:

- (a)(i): substitute `x^2` into the sine series to get `x^2 - x^6/6 + ...`; one A1 per term [2].
- (a)(ii), method 1: square the series from (i), keeping the cross term, to get `x^4 - x^8/3 + ...` (M1 A1 A1).
- (a)(ii), method 2: use `sin^2(x^2) = (1 - cos(2x^2))/2` and expand cosine (M1 A1 A1).
- (b), accepted methods: differentiate the series for `sin^2(x^2)`; use `4x sin(x^2)cos(x^2)=2x sin(2x^2)` and expand; multiply the sine and cosine series; or differentiate the sine series and multiply. Result `4x^3 - 8x^7/3 + ...` [2].
