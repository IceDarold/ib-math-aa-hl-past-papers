# May 2024 TZ1 Paper 3 Question 2(h) — verified transcription

For the quartic `y=x^4-mx^3+nx`, the line through inflection points B and C is
`y=(-m^3/8+n)x`. Show that the other intersection has
`x_A=m/4-(m/4)sqrt(5)` [7].

Markscheme PDF pages 14–16 accepts two routes:

- Method 1: equate the quartic to the line and rearrange to zero (M1). Recognize the known factor associated with C (M1), factor as `x(x-m/2)(x^2-(m/2)x-m^2/4)=0` (A1 M1 A1), then apply the quadratic formula (M1 A1) and select/restated the required root.
- Method 2: after forming the cubic factor, recognize the known root `x_C=m/2`; use sum and product of roots to obtain the quadratic for the other intersections, then use the quadratic formula.
