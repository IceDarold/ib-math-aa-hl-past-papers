# May 2024 TZ1 Paper 1 Question 6 — verified transcription

Question paper, printed page 7 (PDF page 8):

The function is `f(x) = sqrt(x sin(x^2))`, where `0 <= x <= sqrt(pi)`.
Region R is enclosed by the graph of f, the x-axis and `x = sqrt(pi)/2`.
It is rotated by `2pi` radians about the x-axis. Show that the volume is
`pi(2 - sqrt(2))/4`. Maximum mark: 6.

Markscheme, PDF pages 15–16:

- Form the disk-method integral `V = pi integral_0^(sqrt(pi)/2) (f(x))^2 dx` (M1), then substitute `f(x)^2 = x sin(x^2)` (A1).
- Method 1: substitute `u = x^2`, giving `(pi/2) integral_0^(pi/4) sin(u) du`; integrate and evaluate the limits to obtain the required exact value.
- The markscheme also accepts integration by inspection.
- Method 2: substitute `u = cos(x^2)`, with `du/dx = -2x sin(x^2)`; transform the limits and evaluate.
- Total: 6 marks.
