# May 2024 TZ1 Paper 1 Question 10(f) — verified transcription

The earlier parts establish `f(x)=(4x+2)/(x-2)` and `g(x)=x^2-4x-9/4=(x+1/2)(x-9/2)`.

Question: find the product of the solutions of `f(x)=g(x)` [4].

Markscheme PDF pages 25–26:

- Form the equation `(4x+2)/(x-2)=(x+1/2)(x-9/2)` (M1).
- Multiply by `x-2` and form a cubic. The leading term and constant term earn the two intermediate accuracy marks; an equivalent scaled cubic is accepted (A1 A1).
- Use the product-of-roots relation for the cubic to obtain `-5/2` (A1).
- Alternative: observe `x=-1/2` is one solution, reduce to a quadratic whose root product is 5, then multiply by `-1/2`.
